Cell track: press T, click the storm, the view steps back about 15 minutes, click the storm again. Shift-click adds more positions. Line track: press L, click along the line, Enter, then click where the marked center was on the earlier scan. Drag the white polygon's corners to edit it. VROT, max REF and max velocity are simple automatic estimates from the lowest tilt (no dealiasing); the recommendation rules are placeholders to be replaced with your storm-mode and VROT guidance.
